Using my Nextflow pipeline mpCGC, I built mpCGCdb, a web catalog of CAZyme gene clusters (CGCs) that includes sulfatases and lineages beyond Bacteroidota. Rarefaction estimates that current metagenomes capture under 40% of the cluster types in the richest lineages. The enzyme families that share clusters with several uncharacterized sulfatase subfamilies suggest what those sulfatases act on.
