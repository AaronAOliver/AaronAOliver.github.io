Similarity-colocalization networks (SCoNes) group homologous proteins into communities and link communities that repeatedly share gene neighborhoods. Applied to polysaccharide, hydrogenase, nitrogen fixation and natural-product loci across 898 Kiritimatiellia genomes, they point to a lifestyle in which sulfated glycan hydrolysis fuels fermentation.
