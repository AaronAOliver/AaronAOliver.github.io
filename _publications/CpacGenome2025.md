We discovered an extremophile alga in local pond scum that tolerates high heat, pH and salinity, yet is closely related to the model organism <i>Chlamydomonas reinhardtii</i>. It could allow <i>Chlamydomonas</i> to be grown in outdoor ponds without the environmentally harmful chemicals normally used to restrict the growth of competing organisms.
