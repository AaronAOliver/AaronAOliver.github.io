In vitro microcosms built from the hindgut microbiome of <i>Kyphosus cinerascens</i> kept 96 to 99% of the active gut microbial families. Paired metagenomics and metatranscriptomics showed that Gammaproteobacteria first detoxify polyphenols and degrade alginate, then Bacillota, Bacteroidota and Verrucomicrobiota guilds attack the more recalcitrant sulfated polysaccharides.
