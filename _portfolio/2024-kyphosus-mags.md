[Open the interactive notebook](/notebooks/kyphosus-mags.html)

I recovered MAGs from wild *Kyphosus* gut metagenomes and from bioreactors fed
macroalgae, and asked which microbes carry the enzymes to break down red, green
and brown algal polysaccharides. Few genomes can fully degrade any one sulfated
glycan on their own. The work is split across lineages, and many of the
enzymes are exported, which points to joint extracellular degradation.

The notebook covers read-based taxonomy, the PhyloPhlAn tree with enzyme rings,
an interactive explorer for the MAGs, how novel the enzymes are compared with
GenBank, CAZy and SulfAtlas, a GH86 gene tree, and biosynthetic gene cluster
novelty.

- Paper: [Oliver *et al.* 2024, *mBio*](https://doi.org/10.1128/mbio.00496-24)
- Code: [github.com/AaronAOliver/KyphosusMAGs](https://github.com/AaronAOliver/KyphosusMAGs)
- Data: [MAGs on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8277654)
