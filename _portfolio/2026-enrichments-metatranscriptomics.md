[Open the interactive notebook](/notebooks/enrichments-metatranscriptomics.html)

In vitro microcosms seeded with *Kyphosus cinerascens* hindgut contents
reproduce the staged breakdown of seaweed. Gammaproteobacteria first detoxify
polyphenols and degrade alginate, then Bacillota, Bacteroidota and
Verrucomicrobiota guilds attack the sulfated polysaccharides. My
contribution was the genome-resolved expression analysis: mapping metatranscriptomes to 471
dereplicated MAG populations, normalizing and summarizing expression by
enzyme class, and building the figures.

The notebook covers the 471-population phylogeny with gene-count rings, an
explorer for enrichment and enzyme toolkits, expression time series for each
microcosm, MAG-level expression shares, and genomic potential vs. realized
expression.
