In vitro microcosms seeded with *Kyphosus cinerascens* hindgut contents
reproduce the staged breakdown of seaweed. Gammaproteobacteria first detoxify
polyphenols and degrade alginate, then Bacillota, Bacteroidota and
Verrucomicrobiota guilds attack the sulfated polysaccharides.

My contribution was the genome-resolved expression analysis: mapping
metatranscriptomes to 471 dereplicated MAG populations, normalizing and
summarizing expression by enzyme class, and building the figures.

## In the notebook

- A phylogeny of the 471 populations with rings for gene counts
- How much each population was enriched in culture
- Expression of CAZymes, sulfatases and detoxification enzymes over 16 days
- The share of each genome's transcription spent on these enzymes
- Genomic potential vs. realized expression
- Enzyme-level successions from the paper
